Before registering

General donor guidance

India’s e-RaktKosh guidance describes healthy blood donors as generally 18–65 years old and at least 45 kg. Every donor must still complete confidential screening at a licensed blood bank.

18–65General age range
45 kg+General minimum weight
ScreeningRequired before donation
HonestyShare health concerns

This information is educational and does not determine eligibility. Always follow the medical team’s decision at the donation centre.

How RedGold works

A simple connection between donors and people in need

01

Register

A volunteer submits their blood group, location, and contact details with consent.

02

Search

A visitor searches the directory using the required blood group and an optional location.

03

Connect

The visitor contacts a matching donor and coordinates through a licensed hospital or blood bank.

04

Screen

Medical professionals confirm donor eligibility and complete the safe donation process.
